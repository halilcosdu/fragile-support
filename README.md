# Fragile - Privacy & Support

The privacy policy and support page for **Fragile**, a packing puzzle for iPhone and iPad published
by Halil Cosdu.

| Page | URL |
|---|---|
| English | https://halilcosdu.github.io/fragile-support/ |
| Türkçe | https://halilcosdu.github.io/fragile-support/tr.html |

App Store Connect fields:

- Privacy Policy URL (English): `https://halilcosdu.github.io/fragile-support/`
- Privacy Policy URL (Türkçe): `https://halilcosdu.github.io/fragile-support/tr.html`
- Support URL: same page plus `#support`

Two hand-written HTML files, no build step and no framework. They load nothing from a third party -
no web fonts, no scripts, no analytics, no cookies - which is the same standard the app itself is
held to: Fragile collects no data, has no account, shows no ads and makes no network requests.

`wordmark.png` and `icon.png` are rendered from the game's own art so the page matches the app.
