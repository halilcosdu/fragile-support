Inside the game

Swipe or scroll the row to see all six.

The puzzle

One clean way to fit

Slide each toy in, turn it to make the last piece fit, and park one on the tray when it blocks the way. Glass breaks under weight, so keep the heavy toys off it or tuck a foam pad alongside.

Every order ships with a verified three-star solution, so a puzzle is never unfair, and if the box fills up wrong, Undo last and Pack again are always there.

The delivery

The road decides

Seal the box and watch it travel: a smooth road, a bumpy one, or a rough one. Whether everything arrives safe is decided by how you packed it, not by luck.

One star for shipping, two for arriving undamaged, three for packing it tight. The star total decorates your depot: a plant, a workshop banner, a forklift, a delivery van, a neon sign.

The campaign

80 orders, four chapters

Toy Workshop, Rush Hour, Gift Shop and Night Shift, each with its own look and its own trick. On top of that, the Daily Delivery is generated for the calendar date and checked by a solver, so it always has a three-star answer. Keep the streak alive.

Turkish and English throughout, and a settings card where you pick the language, the music, the sound and the haptics.

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 22 August 2026

Fragile does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics service and no third-party code in the app. It makes no network requests of any kind.

Who is responsible

Fragile is developed and published by Halil Cosdu, an individual developer. For anything about this policy, write to cosduhalil@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your device

All of it stays inside the app's own private storage, which only Fragile can read:

Your progressCompleted orders, stars, the daily streak and which tutorial hints you have seen
Your settingsMusic, sound effects, haptics and language
A local play logGameplay events written to a file so a problem can be diagnosed on the device. It holds no personal information, is capped in size and is never uploaded.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app contains no networking code, no advertising or analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind, so there is nothing to send and nowhere for it to go. The game is fully playable in airplane mode.

Third parties

There are none. Fragile has no advertising partners, no analytics provider, no crash reporter and no social or login integrations. Its two typefaces are embedded in the app; nothing is fetched at runtime.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. It contains no advertising, no purchases, no chat, no user-generated content and no links out of the app, and it collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because no personal data is ever collected or transmitted, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export or erase. The data described above lives on your device and is under your control: deleting the app removes all of it, and there is no copy anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

Frequently asked

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Every order, including the Daily Delivery, is generated and checked on your device. The app never goes online.

Are there ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions?

None. The game is free and complete: 80 hand-made orders, four chapters and a new Daily Delivery every day, with nothing to unlock by paying.

Where is my progress stored, and can I move it to another device?

In the app's private storage on that one device. There is no account and no iCloud sync in this version, so progress does not follow you to another iPhone or iPad, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings on the start card and tap the language row. It cycles through Automatic, English and Türkçe; Automatic follows your device language.

Can I turn off the music, the sound or the haptics?

Yes, each one has its own switch on the Settings card. The speaker button in the corner of the game screen mutes everything at once.

Does it run on iPad?

Yes. Fragile is a universal app and runs in portrait on both iPhone and iPad, from iOS 15 onwards.

I am stuck on an order. Is it definitely solvable?

Every order ships with a verified three-star solution, and the Daily Delivery is checked by a solver before it is offered. If the box fills up wrong, the card offers Undo last and Pack again, so the puzzle is never a dead end you cannot leave.
